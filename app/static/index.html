    Floors

    Planes are independent: own map, own fog, own tokens. Players change planes at a stair object; only the DM moves tokens between floors.

    Lights

    Lamps light THEIR plane only, walls block the glow (the same LOS as sight). Toggling here is silent DM administration — players experience the world change, not a fake action. 🕯 toggles room-wide darkness; a token's own light/darkvision lives in its sheet.

    Peek

    The preview is the character's real server-side sight (fog, darkness, light, hidden tokens) — a moment-image; Refresh re-fetches it. Nothing you see here changes what anyone sees.

    How to play

    Views. Tactical is the top-down grid; Diorama is the same room and the same server pathfinding in an isometric table view. Your token, movement and path previews behave identically in both.

    Movement. Click your token, then click a destination: you get a path preview first. Double-click the previewed goal (or press the confirm button) to walk. The walk stops at traps, closed doors, and if you drop to 0 HP.

    Fog of war. Explored terrain stays explored. The DM's 💡 brush toggles room-wide terrain reveal; hidden creatures still need line of sight either way.

    Doors. Click a door next to your token to open or close it. Locked doors need the key — or the DM. Some doors only the DM can move at all (“it won't budge”), and secret passages show up for the DM's map only.

    DM & NPCs. Create a token in 🎲 DM → add a name, click it, and the full statblock editor opens: Identity, Combat, Abilities up top; conditions, defences, spellcasting, attacks, actions and resources in collapsible sections. “＋ Bestiary” saves the block as a template; Encounters spawn saved monsters.

    Sidebar tabs. ⚔ Game (initiative, party) · 👤 Chars (your character) · 📜 Story (quests, journal) · 🎲 DM tools. Chat and dice stay visible always.

    Audio. ▶ starts or resumes the room ambience (self-hosted tracks resume where they were; YouTube/Spotify embeds restart). ⏸ pauses, Stop clears the source.

    Keys. ? help · Esc close / cancel path · WASD or arrows pan the camera · middle/right-drag pans too.

    Anything surprising? Tell your DM — the server decides everything that matters, so what you see is what really happened.
